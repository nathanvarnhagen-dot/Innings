A note from Nate
Thank you for trying out Innings, it means so much that you're willing to try this with some of your time. I've been working to create a better event planner — becoming a way to save the innings we share along the way (corniness inbound).
Where things stand
Innings has a lot of bugs, and many areas aren't nearly fleshed out enough. But I've spent the most time on the idea of an Outside Lands group as an MVP.
🎪
🎪
The OSL group
The goal of the OSL group is an easier way to look at what your festival looks like, the ability to see what artists and stages your friends will be at, and a way to help friends (like me 🙋) hear your favorite songs by each artist — a tap away from Spotify and Apple Music.
👂
👂
Before you dive in
You'll have to reload the page often, there'll be various bugs, and this is nowhere close to perfect. My question to you is: do you see any value in this? If the answer is no, please tell me — and if there's even one feature you liked, I'd appreciate hearing that too. Tell me the good, the bad, and the ugly.
⚾
One inning at a time
As anyone that knows me knows, I spend too much time worried about one inning at a time, only to forget it once the next one starts. I want to remember the innings that came along the way.
